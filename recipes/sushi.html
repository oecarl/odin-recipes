Description

This delicious sushi recipe is very easy to make and you can customise it however you want, which makes it suitable for beginners to make. All of the fillings is wrapped inside the sushi, ensuring a burst of flavour in every bite. Your family and friends will surely love this dish if made with love and care.

Notes:

Storing leftovers: I do not recommend trying to keep leftovers, particularly if you are using raw fish. Even if you do cooked fish and vegetables, once the rice is hard and cold in the fridge, it won't be as enjoyable to eat.

Freezing: Not recommended

Ingredients

  • 6 sheets sushi seaweed
  • 1 batch prepared sushi rice
  • 1/2 lb sashimi-grade raw salmon/desired raw fish of choice
  • 4 oz cream cheese sliced into strips
  • 1 avocado sliced
  • soy sauce for serving

Steps

  1. Place the seaweed on a bamboo mat, then cover the sheet of seaweed with an even layer of prepared sushi rice. Smooth gently with a rice paddle.
  2. Layer salmon, cream cheese, and avocado on the rice, and roll it up tightly. Slice with a sharp knife, and enjoy right away with soy sauce.
